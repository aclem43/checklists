#! Light Signals
> On ground
Green | Authorised to take off*
Green flashing | Authorised to taxi*
Red | Stop
Red flashing | Taxi clear of landing area in use
White flashing | Return to starting point on aerodrome
> In flight
Green | Authorised to land*
Green flashing | Return for landing
Red | Give way to other aircraft and continue circling
Red flashing | Do not land, aerodrome unsafe
> * if pilot is satisfied that no collision risk exists
