=== Cessna 172RG Preflight Checklist · VH-RQU
# Preflight 1. Cabin
POH | Available in airplane
Landing Gear Lever | DOWN
Control Wheel Lock | REMOVE
Ignition Switch | OFF
Avionics Power Switch | OFF
Master Switch | ON
>! Treat propeller as live
Fuel Quantity Indicators | CHECK quantity
Gear Position Light (green) | ILLUMINATED
Avionics Cooling Fan | CHECK audibly
Master Switch | OFF
Fuel Selector Valve | BOTH
Alternate Static Source | OFF
Baggage Door | CHECK secure
# 2. Empennage
Rudder Gust Lock | REMOVE
Tail Tie-Down | DISCONNECT
Control Surfaces | CHECK free & secure
# 3. Right Wing Trailing Edge
Aileron | CHECK free & secure
# 4. Right Wing
Wing Tie-Down | DISCONNECT
Main Wheel Tire | CHECK inflation
Fuel Sumps (tank & selector) | DRAIN, check water/sediment/grade
Fuel Quantity | CHECK visually
Fuel Filler Cap | SECURE, vent clear
> Drain fuel before first flight of day & after each refuel
---
# 5. Nose
Static Source Openings | CHECK clear
Engine Oil Level | Min 5 qt, 8 qt for extended flight
Fuel Strainer | DRAIN ~4 sec, check closed
Propeller and Spinner | CHECK nicks, security, oil leaks
Landing Lights | CHECK condition
Nose Gear Doors | CHECK secure
Nose Strut and Tire | CHECK inflation
Nose Tie-Down | DISCONNECT
# 6. Left Wing
Main Wheel Tire | CHECK inflation
Fuel Tank Sump | DRAIN, check water/sediment/grade
Fuel Quantity | CHECK visually
Fuel Filler Cap | SECURE
# 7. Left Wing Leading Edge
Pitot Tube Cover | REMOVE, check clear
Fuel Tank Vent | CHECK clear
Stall Warning Vane | CHECK (master ON, horn)
Wing Tie-Down | DISCONNECT
# 8. Left Wing Trailing Edge
Aileron | CHECK free & secure
! Remove all frost, ice & snow · Night: check lights, carry torch

=== Cessna 172RG Checklist · VH-RQU

# Before Starting Engine
Preflight Inspection | COMPLETE
Seats, Belts, Harnesses | ADJUST & LOCK
Fuel Selector Valve | BOTH
Avionics, Autopilot, Elec. Equip. | OFF
>! Avionics OFF for start to prevent damage
Brakes | TEST & SET
Cowl Flaps | OPEN
Landing Gear Lever | DOWN
Circuit Breakers | CHECK IN
# Starting Engine
Mixture | RICH
Propeller | HIGH RPM
Carburetor Heat | COLD
Throttle | PUMP 1–2×, open ¼"
> Hot engine: aux fuel pump ON during start
!! PROPELLER AREA CLEAR!
Master Switch | ON
Ignition Switch | START, release when started
Oil Pressure | CHECK
Beacon & Nav Lights | ON as required
Avionics Power Switch | ON
Radios | ON
---
# Before Takeoff
Parking Brake | SET
Cabin Doors | CLOSED & LOCKED
Flight Controls | FREE & CORRECT
Flight Instruments | SET
Fuel Selector Valve | BOTH
Mixture | RICH (below 3000 ft)
Aux Fuel Pump | ON (pressure rise), then OFF
Elevator & Rudder Trim | TAKEOFF
Throttle | 1800 RPM
~ a | Magnetos: max drop 150 RPM, max diff 50 RPM
~ b | Propeller: CYCLE high–low, return HIGH
~ c | Carb Heat: CHECK for RPM drop
~ d | Engine Instruments & Ammeter: CHECK
~ e | Suction Gage: CHECK
Throttle | 1000 RPM or less
Radios | SET
Autopilot (if installed) | OFF
Strobe Lights | AS DESIRED
Throttle Friction Lock | ADJUST
Parking Brake | RELEASE

===
# Normal Takeoff
Wing Flaps | 0°
Carburetor Heat | COLD
Power | FULL THROTTLE & 2700 RPM
Elevator Control | LIFT NOSE WHEEL at 55 KIAS
Climb Speed | 70–80 KIAS
Brakes | APPLY momentarily when airborne
Landing Gear | RETRACT in climb out
# Short Field Takeoff
Wing Flaps | 0°
Carburetor Heat | COLD
Brakes | APPLY
Power | FULL THROTTLE & 2700 RPM
Brakes | RELEASE
Elevator Control | Slightly tail-low attitude
Climb Speed | 63 KIAS until obstacles cleared
Landing Gear | RETRACT after obstacles cleared
# Normal Climb
Airspeed | 85–95 KIAS
Power | 25" Hg & 2500 RPM
Fuel Selector Valve | BOTH
Mixture | FULL RICH (lean above 3000 ft)
Cowl Flaps | OPEN as required
# Maximum Performance Climb
Airspeed | 84 KIAS SL → 77 KIAS 10,000 ft
Power | FULL THROTTLE & 2700 RPM
Fuel Selector Valve | BOTH
Mixture | FULL RICH (lean above 3000 ft)
Cowl Flaps | FULL OPEN
---
# Cruise
Power | 15–25" Hg, 2100–2700 RPM
Elevator & Rudder Trim | ADJUST
Mixture | LEAN
Cowl Flaps | CLOSED
# Descent
Fuel Selector Valve | BOTH
Power | AS DESIRED
Carburetor Heat | FULL HEAT as required
Mixture | ENRICHEN as required
Cowl Flaps | CLOSED
Wing Flaps | AS DESIRED
> Flaps 0–10° below 130 KIAS, 10–30° below 100 KIAS
> Gear may be extended below 140 KIAS to descend faster
# Before Landing
Seats, Belts, Harnesses | ADJUST & LOCK
Fuel Selector Valve | BOTH
Landing Gear | DOWN (below 140 KIAS)
Landing Gear | CHECK main gear down, green light
Mixture | RICH
Carburetor Heat | ON (before closing throttle)
Propeller | HIGH RPM
Autopilot (if installed) | OFF

===
# Normal Landing
Airspeed | 65–75 KIAS (flaps UP)
Wing Flaps | AS DESIRED
Airspeed | 60–70 KIAS (flaps DOWN)
Trim | ADJUST
Touchdown | MAIN WHEELS FIRST
Landing Roll | LOWER NOSE WHEEL GENTLY
Braking | MINIMUM REQUIRED
# Short Field Landing
Airspeed | 65–75 KIAS (flaps UP)
Wing Flaps | 30° (below 100 KIAS)
Airspeed | MAINTAIN 63 KIAS
Trim | ADJUST
Power | REDUCE to idle as obstacle cleared
Touchdown | MAIN WHEELS FIRST
Brakes | APPLY HEAVILY
Wing Flaps | RETRACT for max braking
#! Balked Landing
Power | FULL THROTTLE & 2700 RPM
Carburetor Heat | COLD
Wing Flaps | RETRACT to 20°
Climb Speed | 55 KIAS
Wing Flaps | RETRACT slowly after 65 KIAS
Cowl Flaps | OPEN
---
# After Landing
Wing Flaps | UP
Carburetor Heat | COLD
Cowl Flaps | OPEN
# Securing Airplane
Parking Brake | SET
Throttle | 1000 RPM
Avionics & Elec. Equipment | OFF
Mixture | IDLE CUT-OFF
Throttle | CLOSE as RPM drops
Ignition Switch | OFF
Master Switch | OFF
Control Lock | INSTALL
